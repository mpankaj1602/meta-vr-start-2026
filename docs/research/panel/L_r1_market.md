## Findings

**Prior art.** Rambling aloud into tasks is a mainstream phone feature: Todoist Ramble, January 2026 [1]; Tiimo, iPhone App of the Year 2025, sells an AI "brain dump" [2]; Sunsama has a guided daily shutdown [3]. Vision Pro has about seven sticky-note apps, one voice-created (Parchi) [4]; Quest's Sticky Space has too few reviews to rate [5]. None picks three, none ends, none has a character. Expressive lamps exist as hardware: Apple's ELEGNT research lamp, Ongo ($299) [12][13]. No lamp agent found in XR.

**Lamp.** Coverage of ELEGNT and Ongo is headlined "Pixar-style" [12][13]; judges will react likewise, and the rules bar third-party IP. The mascot is modelled on Luxo's lamp; Luxo sued Disney in 2009 over lamp merchandise, and the settlement left "artistic renditions" alone [14]. Legal risk looks low (no action against lamp robots found); originality risk is high. A banker's lamp (1909: brass stand, glass shade, pull chain) is a different object and already fits the spec's chain [15].

**Name.** No Quest title found. Steam has Lamplight City [16]. Android has "Lamplight: Mood Journal", private and on-device: same category and privacy pitch [17].

**Demand.** Scullin: 57 adults; to-do writers fell asleep faster, more so with specific lists [10]; Masicampo and Baumeister as cited [11]. Voice assistants (2016 survey): 39% use at home, 3% in public, 1.3% at work [18]: fine for a home wind-down, not for a plane or a judge's office. Vision Pro: 41% daily, productivity leading [19]. No Quest time-of-day data found; the evening ritual is unevidenced and a morning headset session less plausible (inference).

**Field.** 2025 gallery, my count over titles and taglines of 653 entries [7]: 66 mention AI; 13 are AI assistants or companions, none won; about six notes, task or reminder apps, none won. AI winners: Chores.gg, Flows.xr, Language IRL, and The Nanauts (WebXR: "hands, voice, scene understanding and Llama 3.3"). 2026: 2,561 registered with six weeks left (2025: 2,865, yielding 655 entries); gallery unpublished [20]. Meta lists a session, "Agentic Workflows for New & Existing Apps": "what an agentic interaction looks like when it's actually useful, where it tends to break" [21]. The rules name ChatGPT wrappers [20]. Expect many assistants (inference).

**Falls apart elsewhere?** No. Speak, get cards, pick three works on a phone [1]; only desk persistence, hand sorting and the lamp's presence are native. Not a Boldest Original candidate. Routes: Productivity, Agentic, Accessibility ("voice alternatives for hand interactions") [22].

**Scores as written.** Win 6, level with Three Stones: more routes, more ways to lose marks. Feasibility 3, Difficulty 4: voice chain, uptime to 11 December, a character that must feel alive. With the changes below, 7 (judgment).

## Keep / cut / change / add

1. **Keep** one loop: speak, cards, pick three by hand, lights out; the track says "task management ... wind-down" [20].
2. **Keep** silent mode as Core; make preset cards the judge's default [18][9].
3. **Change** the lamp to a banker's lamp expressing through shade tilt and light; no sprung arm, no hop [12][13][14][15].
4. **Change** the name, or add a subtitle [16][17].
5. **Promote** "one question" and "suggest three" to Core. Without them the model only transcribes: a wrapper [20]. With them it is a "spatial agent" giving "context-aware assistance" [22]. In 2025 assistants lost; embodied characters won [7].
6. **Cut** weekly review, streak lights, cosmetic growth, time chips, phrasing memory, head nod. Broad notes apps won nothing [7]; Meta is teaching "Kill Your Darlings: Scope Your Build" [21].
7. **Change** the return moment to the next evening, reviewing yesterday's three first; morning optional. Mainstream adults buy the headset as a TV replacement [23].
8. **Add** an optional private link showing tomorrow's three outside the headset; it answers "a phone is faster" [1].
9. **Keep** the offline fallback and the sample-data judge path, and show both in the video; the rules state the take-it-away test [20].

**Positioning.** "Say it once, sort it by hand, lights out." Unlike a chat assistant, the lamp never converses: it listens once, lays cards on your desk, points, and you decide. Unlike a notes app, it keeps three cards and it ends.
