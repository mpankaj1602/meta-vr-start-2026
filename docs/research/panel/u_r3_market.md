## Disputes

1. **Designer: "store traction is not the rubric."** Half right. Originality is a quarter of the score, and a judge can find two hand-tracked cube tutors on Quest (2.7 stars, 12 reviews; unrated) plus an IWSDK build the spec admits "may be in this field". Nothing comparable exists for sudoku. Traction is also the only evidence on return.
2. **Designer: R1-R level with N3 at 5.** N3 had no headset rival; R1-R has two. Not level.
3. **Designer: R1-R above D1-R.** I hold them equal at 4. R1-R opens better and its par puzzle is new. D1-R fails gently (shallow); R1-R fails as wrong-layer turns in front of a judge, the fault the one cube review names, untestable without a headset.
4. **Accessibility Forward for R1-R.** The award names voice alternatives and eyes-only navigation; the spec has neither.
5. **Reason to Come Back for D1-R.** The designer found every tested cube falls to single-step logic, so nothing grows between sessions. Weak.

## Concessions

- **D1: Win 4 to 3, Feasibility 4 to 3, Difficulty 3 to 4.** The user's sizes take thirty to ninety minutes (engineer); a second review says sudoku does not need 3D (red team); hands only point as written.
- **R1 name note was incomplete.** The red team found a US trade dress on the black-bodied cube: the look matters too. No score change.
- **R1-R earns 4, one above R1.** Par puzzles and previewed turns answer the review complaint; I found neither shipped.
