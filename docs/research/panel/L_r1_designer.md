## 1. First five minutes (judge alone, Quest 3, empty storage)

**Voice path as written.**
- 0:00 **A:** lamp lands wrong (no room scan, monitor in the way); "palms flat" is a chore before any payoff.
- 0:15 **B:** a thin chain at 0.65 m is beyond Meta's 42-46 cm touch zone [HANDS] and a small ray target.
- 0:25 **C:** two talk triggers, both a reach. **D:** microphone prompt mid-scene. **E:** blank mind, open office, "where does my voice go?": the embarrassment peak.
- 0:30 **F:** when am I done? A long held pinch breaks when tracking blinks.
- 0:50 **G:** dead air. **H:** wrong cards: misheard names, seven from a ramble, a worry rewritten as a task.
- 1:10 **I:** five gestures in two minutes; a resting palm stamps; fists hide fingers (inference, [GEST]).
- 2:00 **J:** "see a week" is a stranger's data.

**Silent path as written.** A, B, then a three-way capture choice (**K**), jittery scrawl (**L**) and a lamp with nothing to do (**M**).

**Default: hand first, voice second.** 0:00 the lamp floats ahead; pinch-slide it down until your hand meets the desk; a ghost hand shows how, not text [GEST]. 0:10 chain, same verb. 0:20 five starter cards fan out; slide three under the lamp. 0:45 a mic spot lights: "Or just say it." Permission is asked only then, so voice failure lands on a working desk. Inference: a permission prompt before any value is the likeliest exit.

## 2. Sort

**Must:** chain wake and lights-out (bookends; teach the verb); cards persist where left (passthrough claim); voice capture (the concept); voice-level light; cards in the user's own words, six or fewer (trust); slide to slots, later tray, let go (core); lamp spotlights its three (agency without speech or delay; promoted); preset cards (zero-permission start); morning wake, done, done stack (the return); one-hand and mirrored layouts (free with one verb); pointer plus pinch everywhere; captions, large high-contrast type by default, nothing by colour alone; memo card on failure; push-to-talk, privacy line, no advice; passthrough dimming; floating tray; anchor with one-gesture re-place; preview on own cards.
**Should:** one question, answerable by pinch (visible reasoning); carry-over curl with shrink-or-let-go offer (best agent moment); morning beam sweep; voice-only sorting, four verbs (Accessibility wording); system keyboard.
**Could:** palm stamp (contact is uncertain from headset cameras [TOUCH]); audio playback on cards; plane detection.
**Cut:** fist crumple (second verb); rewriting into next steps (puts words in mouths); type marks (need a legend); time chips (second decision); head nod (misfires); handwriting (lamp cannot read it; my favourite; revive after a one-day device test); spoken lamp voice; weekly review, streak lights, cosmetic wear, phrasing memory (not felt in one sitting); agent tools cut to four.

## 3. The lamp

A fixed stem, a hooded shade that turns, a chain; no sprung arm, hop, nod, bow or face. Press called Apple's research lamp Pixar-style on sight [YANKO]; an articulated hopping lamp invites the same and costs originality marks. In that study expressive motion helped social tasks, made no significant difference in functional ones, and some disliked movement "for no apparent reasons" and a stiff voice [ELEGNT]. So the lamp moves only to point and never speaks aloud.

Vocabulary: asleep (chain glows); awake (pool); listening (light follows your voice, a ring runs down, earcon [VOICE]); thinking (slow breath while blank cards slide out); pointing (beam narrows on one card: suggestion, question, brief); unsure (card in pencil, dashed edge); error (one dip, low tone, memo card). Captions sit beside the lit card; one question and one suggestion a session.

## 4. Voice

Start: one pinch on the mic spot, not a hold (sustained pinching tires [HANDS]). Stop: pinch again, a pause, or the ring running out. Understood: your words appear as an ink line at the lamp's foot (Meta pairs listening with level and live transcript [ATTN]), then tear into cards. Wrong card: pinch it and say it again; only that card changes. Failure never dead-ends [GOOG]: the first miss becomes a memo card; on the second the preset fan slides out unasked. Mic refused or server down: no mic spot.

## 5. Hands and desk

Two actions: pinch-slide and pinch-tap. Done is a slide onto a pad where a stamp falls; let go is a slide past the pool's edge, where the card crumples itself. The desk gives a forearm rest, friction and a hard stop, which improve precision and confidence [SURF]; it is never a touch sensor. Layout fits a tray table. No desk: the same layout on a slanted floating tray, pointer-driven, forearms on armrests.

## 6. Return

Session 2 opens on yesterday's cards: retire before capture, no teaching. Session 7: a thick stack, a curled twice-carried card, the shrink offer, ninety seconds. In one sitting: lights out, a ten-second night, the judge's own three waiting, labelled "Preview: tomorrow". "Preview: day 7" curls one of their cards and stacks their stamped card on outlined blanks, not invented achievements. Exit restores tonight. Reopening the URL next day is the real proof.

## 7. Additions (rubric or award; cost)

1. Hand-first start (First Five Minutes; five naive playtests).
2. One verb everywhere (Technical reliability; drag-feel tuning on device).
3. Desk for feel, tray-table size (seat test, FoV; resting-hand tracking check).
4. Ink line tearing into cards (Agentic, voice-first; delay tuning).
5. Six-word reason on each lamp act, such as "carried twice" (Agentic, context-aware; rule tuning).
6. Preview on own cards (Reason to Come Back, video honesty; authoring).
7. Sound as haptics: scrape tied to hand speed, slot knock, stamp (Polish; original foley).
8. Three complete routes, pointer-only, one-hand, voice-only, tested with disabled users before claiming (Accessibility Forward; recruiting).
