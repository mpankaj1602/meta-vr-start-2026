## S2

- **Head-steered dodging ships, visually.** Wrong Way on a One Way (Vision Pro): tilt the head to steer a motorcycle through traffic [2, summary]. RidePods (iPhone, September 2025): head tilt read from AirPods steers a bike through traffic [1]. Temple Run VR (Gear VR, 2014) used head tilts [5, summary].
- **Head-tracked sound as the core exists off-headset and in prototypes.** Blinded (iPhone plus AirPods Pro): eyes closed, turn your head to the sound [3]. Bose's audio glasses had about 30 games using nods and head turns [8, summary]. Quest: Blackout Protocol VR, sideload only [7]; research: Legend of Iris [12]. No store title found.
- **New to headsets?** As a combination, yes: none found on Quest, Vision Pro or WebXR.
- **"Would fall apart on any other platform"?** Still no. Head-tracked spatial audio plus head input is what earbuds already give a phone [1][3]; dropping hands removes the one sensor earbuds lack (inference).
- **Demand.** Eagle Flight, steered wholly by the head, reviewed well, with no neck strain after 30 minutes because movements are graceful, not jerky [4, summary]. Reaction nods are jerky (inference). Head input is an established alternative for people who cannot use hands [15] and a documented barrier for limited neck mobility [16]. No evidence that blind players ask for it.
- **Fit.** Meta's messaging is hands-first [17]; a game whose hands only open the menu reads off-brief (inference).

## M1

- **First-person VR runners and mazes exist and review badly on comfort.** Temple Run VR: a Game Informer writer got cold sweats and wrote "I never want to try it again" [6]; its makers said camera movement the player did not cause provokes nausea [13, summary]. Maze Walk VR: the reviewer warns of queasiness [11, summary]. Dreadhalls, the known first-person VR maze, is rated "intense" for comfort [10, summary]. projectJUPITER_a, an App Lab maze: 2 reviews [18].
- **What works.** Pistol Whip moves the player forward in a straight line at constant speed, designed for comfort [19]; a maze adds turns.
- **Hand-tracked or head-steered first-person maze on Quest:** none found. Hand-tracked mazes are tabletop [14].
- **Demand.** None found from seated mainstream adults. "Turn physically when standing" leaves the seated brief. M1 gives up the tabletop form that made G5-R the safest build.
