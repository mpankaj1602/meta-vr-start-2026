## 2. Disputes

1. **N1 as "Boldest Original Concept" (designer, r2 L10 and L85; r1_designer L41, Win 6).** The designer's own risk note says a phone could imitate part of it. Worse, the rule is not original: Frozen In Fear: Don't Blink is on Quest, Keep Watching VR adds the flashlight and headphones, Strike Eye VR freezes enemies on look (URLs in section 1). The award is "judged on originality". What survives is tone (cozy, seated, hands-only). Change: N1 Win 4; award target moved to First Five Minutes.
2. **A1 at Win 6 (judge r1 L20; engineer r1 L86; red team r1 L20).** I hold 4 for A1 and give A1-R 5. New evidence makes the genre more crowded than round one showed: Stringscape and Reed Laser Harp already ship pinch-pluck strings, on top of Maestro, Blooming Conductor, HandJam, GlitterDark, PatchWorld and four 2025 winners. The judge's own caveat is that rivals "tend to be audio professionals". Judges liking music is real, which is why I do not go lower.
3. **"Productivity is the thinnest field" (judge r1 L95; designer r1 L281).** Agree on direction, not on degree, and nobody has counts: the gallery is unpublished and shows only 2,393 registrants (https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/project-gallery). For: no productivity title in the Glasses slate, Workrooms closed, lowest prize. Against: two of the three 2025 camera + AI winners were task apps (Chores.gg, Flows.xr), that category is gone, so those teams and their imitators land in Productivity; "creative tools" pulls in music and art tools; assistant-style LLM entries chasing the Agentic award will mostly file here (inference). Change: A4 and A4-R stay at 6, not the judge's 7.
4. **P7-R as the pick over A4-R (designer r2 L247).** Librarium shows the idea has been tried on Quest without becoming a staple, Enhance VR (same "daily brain workout" promise) sits at 92 reviews, and the designer accepts the reassignment risk. In Gaming it is one puzzle among many. I do credit one point against the red team (dispute 9). Change: P7-R Win 5, below A4-R.
5. **G5 refined "would rise to about Win 6" (judge r1 L143); G5-R Feasibility 5 as a reason to rank it high (designer r2 L234).** Three hand-tracked maze products exist, one free and one in the browser. Daily habits with evidence are word and logic puzzles: NYT reported 11.2 billion solves in 2025, 4.2 billion of them Wordle and 612 million for the logic game Pips (https://www.nbcnewyork.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/hardest-wordle-connections-games-2025/6436464/). I found no daily maze anywhere. Change: G5-R Win 4; N2 ranks above it.
6. **"The market analyst found only a word game and a Wordle port doing daily on Quest", used to justify three daily entries (designer r2 L212).** That finding shows thin supply, not demand for any daily. The demand evidence is specific to word and deduction formats (dispute 5; Murdle; Clues by Sam). Change: of the dailies, only N2 gets credit for a proven habit.
7. **P3 at Win 5 (judge r1 L15) and P3-R targeting First Five Minutes (designer r2 L16).** Space Golf and Generally Relative Space Golf are VR gravity-golf games with Quest builds, LinksWorld does drag-release slingshots round a black hole on a tabletop, and Tiny Golf won on the same verb. Titans of Space PLUS (4.7★, hand-tracked) owns the tour. Change: P3 stays 3; P3-R 4 (better scoped, not novel).
8. **A6 at Win 6 (engineer r1 L91; red team r1 L25).** The Line (Emmy 2020) and Paper Birds are hand-tracked paper or diorama stories made by studios, so the form is not new and the comparison set is professional. No reason to return, and the special award it targets is open to both divisions (judge r1 L49). Change: A6 Win 4. A6-R gets 5 because sourcing public-domain art removes the entrant's biggest gap; the public-domain claim checks out.
9. **"What remains is a generic daily puzzle set" (red team r1 L19, on P7).** Meta's own segment research says the under-represented Leisure Lovers play "puzzle, strategy, rhythm", have the shortest sessions, and come back for self-improvement feedback (https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/know-your-players-understanding-your-audience-with-2025-quest-gamer-segments/). A recall score is exactly that feedback, without any health claim. Change: none to P7 as written (2), but P7-R holds at 5 rather than dropping to the red team's implied level.
10. **E3-R as a strong Accessibility play (designer r2 L13; engineer r1 L107 for E3).** TRIPP ships a hand-played Sound Bath on Quest and Lungy does it on Vision Pro, so the wind-down half is taken by wellness brands. The sight-free half cannot be tested with blind users in six weeks (designer's own note, r2 L119), and the award pool includes Adapted titles adding accessibility modes. Meditation apps also retain poorly: Calm 8.34% and Headspace 7.65% at day 30 (https://apptopia.com/blog/we-know-you-didnt-keep-your-new-years-resolutions/). Change: E3-R Win 4.

## 5. Per-track ranking

Would the mainstream-adult Glasses buyer come back? My read, with the evidence available:

- **N2 Alibi: most likely.** Daily word and logic puzzles are the one short habit with mass proof (11.2 billion NYT solves in 2025).
- **P7-R Pocket Palace: sometimes.** Fits what Meta says Leisure Lovers return for; the brain-workout category has not held users on Quest (Enhance VR).
- **A4-R Three Stones: unlikely as a standalone.** Vision Pro task apps are used because they sync with a tool people already live in; this one lives only in the headset and the designer concedes a phone is faster. It returns only if the headset is already on for work or television.
- **A7-R Paper Trail: once, happily.** Two cases, then done.
- **N3 Snip: occasionally.** Something to make and send; seasonal pull during a late-November judging window (inference).
- **A1-R, E3-R: rarely.** Ambient and wellness apps retain under 10% at a month.
- **A6-R, P3-R, P5-R: no.** Finite content.
- **A3-R, A2-R: only with a friend, or a working model.**

**Overall recommendation: A4-R Three Stones.**
- **Main reason:** it is the only entry that matches the Productivity track's own sentence, in the track most likely to be weakest, and my search found no headset product doing this ritual. It is also among the cheapest to finish.
- **Main risk:** it is slight. A judge is done in three minutes, real users would probably not come back daily without phone sync, and last year's task-app winners (Chores.gg, Flows.xr) show that better-equipped native teams enter this space. If the panel doubts it can carry a session, N3 Snip is the Productivity alternative with a cleaner "nothing like it in a headset" claim, and N2 Alibi is the entry with the best evidence that people return.
