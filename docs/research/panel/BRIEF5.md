## The task for this round

This is not a ranking exercise. The user has asked for the concept to be
**evaluated and tweaked on the basis of research**, ending in a final concept
with requirements, non-requirements and functionality. The user's instruction:
"focus on high level tweaks; for low level or two-way decisions we will see when
we work on it." So:

- Work at the level of what the product is, what it must and must not do, which
  features are core, and which engine. Do not specify thresholds, layouts, exact
  gestures or library choices unless one is a make-or-break risk.
- Every recommended change must come with its reason and, where it rests on a
  fact, a source.
- Say what to **keep, cut, change and add**. The spec is deliberately
  feature-rich; cutting is as valuable as adding.
- Stay honest about the earlier lessons: do not declare things impossible
  without a source; do not remove what makes the concept itself.

Scores use the BRIEF4 definitions: Win 1-10, Feasibility 1-5 (likelihood of a
polished, comfortable, compliant result by 18 Nov given risks AI coding does not
compress), Difficulty 1-5 (how hard it is to get the experience right).

Ground rules as before: cite URLs; label inference; invent nothing; do not
modify the project repository; do not spawn other agents; keep reports dense.
