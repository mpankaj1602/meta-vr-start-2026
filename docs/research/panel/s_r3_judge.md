# S1 and S1-R, round 3: Competition Judge

## Disputes

1. **Designer: S1-R ranks "above E3-R".** Both claim the same two award phrases. S1-R is still a timing game; E3-R needs "no pinch, no precision", closer to "inclusive by default". S1-R's edge: every judge plays by ear without opting in. Net: level.
2. **Designer: claims "high-contrast modes".** The spec defines none; Keep the light is a sound-off mode. I credit one phrase, "inclusive by default". Head-lean, the only limited-mobility path, is "first to go if late".
3. **Designer: passthrough answers Technical.** Partly. Passthrough is scored on whether the "real room meaningfully changes the experience"; a dimmer treats every room alike, and alpha dimming is unconfirmed. Technical 5 to 6 only. Polish is fixed more fully, 4 to 6: no black frame, a filmable arc, captions.
4. **Skeptic: fixed, "it reaches 4, no higher".** Every fix the skeptic listed is in the spec, and the same unknowns (speakers, latency, no blind testers) sat under N1 and E3-R at 4.6. Consistency gives 5.
5. **Market, designer: S1 above or level with E3.** The engineer's evidence cuts the other way: motions "parallel to the view (such as chops)" track worst, and Quest 3S has no headphone jack. A stated mechanic that fails on the judging device does not beat an unstated one.

## Concessions

- S1 Accessibility fit: partial to weak. Skeptic: the screen reader is "experimental" and controller-only; deaf players lose their only channel.
- S1 name: unflagged to content-rule risk. Market, skeptic: "Sonic" on a runner.
- S1-R forecast: 25-26/40 to 25/40. Designer: Technical is "one wrist joint and a dimmer".
- S1-R against E3-R on the award: weaker to level (designer's default-path point).
- S1 numbers unchanged: Win 4, Feasibility 3, Difficulty 3.

## Final scores

S1-R passes stage one. Criteria 6/7/6/6 = 25/40 (S1: 6/5/5/4 = 20). Left-right only is enough game for a ten-minute session and fits "scope to one mechanic"; one three-way choice every two seconds with no fail state will not place in Gaming.
