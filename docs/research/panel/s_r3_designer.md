# Round 3: designer's final scores

## Anticipated attacks (S1-R)

- **Judge: "Dimming my room is passthrough as decoration, and dark-as-reward is still a blindfold."** Partly answered. The dimmer is the difficulty dial and the hit feedback; I claim no room awareness. The blindfold point half lands.
- **Engineer: "Ten features and two inputs behind device checks, with no headset."** Partly answered. Both inputs run in the emulator; head-lean and passthrough are cut first. Resting-hand tracking has no answer until day one.
- **Skeptic: "The headset is unnecessary, a blind player cannot start it, and nobody blind will test it in one."** No answer. The claim is worded to what gets built.
- **Market: "Three phone games do this, audio-first games earn praise, not players, and Adapted titles share the award pool."** No answer. Only "new to headsets" survives.
