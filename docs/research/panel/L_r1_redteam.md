Scores: SPEC_V1 as written. Trimmed as below: 6/4/3, level with A4-R; 7 once voice is proven on device.

## 1. Judge session
- Microphone permission must be granted before entering the session [4]. Inference from [1]: a judge says "buy milk", gets "Buy milk", sees dictation.
- 2020 commercial recognisers: 19% word error for white, 35% for Black speakers [5]; newer models better (unquantified).
- Service down: "voice memo, generic title" looks broken exactly when judged.
- No desk: the resting-hand slide has no floating-tray equivalent; camera tracking of surface contact is weak at oblique, occluded angles [6].
- Silent path: only presets are reliable, which is Three Stones with a lamp. Sound, but no AI.
- Visible in five minutes: about eight of thirty features.

## 2. AI
- Concession: the award lists "spatial agents, voice-first AI interaction ... context-aware assistance" [3]; Lamplight fits three. A fallback does not disprove "core"; I drop my A2-R argument.
- Weakness: speech-to-tasks ships on phones [7], and the rules name ChatGPT wrappers [3]. Only the spotlight suggestion and the one question exceed a phone; both are ranked "Should".
- Whisper (2023) invented phrases in about 1% of transcripts, 38% harmful, more with long pauses [8]; summarisers hallucinate 1.8% to 15% [9].
- Contradiction: titles "written as a concrete next step" versus "only reformats ... no advice". A worry has no next step until the model invents one.
- "Worry" and "person" marks label feelings and invite names; projects "may not mention ... any identifiable person", nor brand names [3].

## 3. Privacy
- Microphone data is "Device User Data": public privacy policy, written limits on service providers [10] (browser scope not stated). Voice SDK terms require explicit consent before collection [11].
- "Nothing retained on the server" can be false: OpenAI keeps abuse logs up to 30 days on chat endpoints without approved zero retention [12]. False statements are "misleading" [3].
- Audio snippets keep raw recordings on a shareable headset (inference).

## 4. Claims and moment
- Scullin: 57 young adults, one lab night, pen and paper [13]. Light-emitting readers delayed sleep about ten minutes versus print [14]; headset light suppressed melatonin in 24 of 32 under lab stimuli [15]. Counter: bedtime VR relaxation shortened onset about six minutes in 29 adolescents [16]. Mixed; nothing transfers.
- Meta: "Do not use during sleep or as a sleep aid" [17]; health claims barred [18]. Citing Scullin implies one.
- Safer: end of work day. Planning unfinished goals then improved detachment in 103 employees [19]; Masicampo holds without sleep [20]. Keep the track's word "wind-down" [3]; drop bed.

## 5. Lamp
The studio's lamp is its mascot; the lamp maker sued in 2009 and settled [21]. Inference: a generic lamp is lawful, but hop, head tilt and bow are that character's performance, and judges will compare. Users' expectations of agents run "dramatically out of step" with capability [22].

## 6. Scope
Eight gesture recognisers against "teach one core interaction" [23] and "one mechanic" [24]. A fist hides fingers [25]; a false crumple destroys a card.

## 7. Usefulness
A phone is faster and needs no headset at 7 am; Meta's VR chief said (2023) newer buyers were "just not as into it" [26]. Survives: a ritual with a physical end, when the headset is already on.

## Keep / cut / change / add
1. Keep the spine: chain, speak, cards, three slots, lights out, cards waiting, press done.
2. Keep push-to-talk, captions, "model proposes, app executes", sample-data judge path.
3. Add "hear a sample evening": entrant-recorded, cached, labelled, offline.
4. Change cards to the user's words, extracted not rewritten, with undo; next steps only from the user's answer.
5. Promote the question and spotlight suggestion to Core.
6. Change the lamp to light, not limbs: beam, warmth, flicker.
7. Silent path: presets plus keyboard; cut handwriting.
8. Cut fist crumple, palm stamp, fan-out, nod, spoken sorting.
9. Cut time chips, weekly review, streak, growth, memory, ageing, lamp speech, audio snippets, type marks.
10. Change the moment to end of day, cards waiting "next time you sit down"; no bed, sleep or Scullin anywhere.
11. Make the privacy line exact and vendor-true, with a policy page.
12. Add device gates: microphone in session, time to first card on poor Wi-Fi, five accented speakers, three unfamiliar rooms. If voice fails, ship silent.

## Design out
1. Voice declined or broken in the first minute.
2. A wrong, invented or presumptuous card.
3. Reading as "famous lamp, chatbot wrapper, sleep gadget" within ten seconds.
