# S2, M1: skeptic round 3

Bare line numbers refer to `t_r2_refined.md`.

## Attack on the refined entries

**S2-R Echo Wood: SERIOUS.** Holds: position-only head input; three-way height coding with fallback; never fully black; 6 cm moves are safe and pass the seat test. Dodging does not corrupt the cue: 6 cm shifts a source 2 m away by under 2° (my arithmetic).
1. **That cuts both ways.** The sound does not audibly answer a lean (inference), so head position is a sensor, not an experience. Tilt and taps reproduce the loop on earbuds (`t_r1_market.md` L10-12). Boldest is weak, and Accessibility was dropped (L55): no award route.
2. **Catching is two buttons.** It clears stage one (`t_r1_judge.md` L8), but a timed pinch earns few "hand tracking" marks, and knock-means-away plus chime-means-toward doubles the rules in minute one (designer's own risk, L65).
3. **Thresholds.** A 3 cm rise sits near ordinary shifting in an office chair (inference). Four deciding facts are device-only (L61).

**M1-R Homing: SERIOUS, not worth building.** Holds: held look, junction-only snaps, no standing. Open, by the designer's own account: looking and steering share the head; comfort is unprovable before a device; earbuds run its core. No first-person version deserves building: each comfortable one is dark and flat or becomes Lantern.

**Survives overall:** S2-R's height coding grafted onto S1-R's hand input.

## Disputes

1. **Designer: Boldest "partly" (L104).** The award is "judged on ... whether the experience would fall apart on any other platform" (BRIEF2.md). It would not (point 1).
2. **Engineer: S2 Win 4** (`t_r1_engineer.md` L39). Its own row says "hands idle in play", and a strict judge could fail it (`t_r1_judge.md` L8). I hold 3.
3. **Market: M1 Win 3.** Its own evidence ("I never want to try it again", `t_r1_market.md` L17) supports 2.

## Concessions

- M1 fix (pinch hops, no auto-run): withdrawn; it renames Lantern (designer, L103). M1-R scored as specified.
- "Sound that answers your own movement": withdrawn at 6 cm.
- S2 Feasibility 4, now 3: comfort and height legibility are device-only (`t_r1_engineer.md` L21, L44).
- S1 and S1-R: scores unchanged (3, 4). Withdrawn: S1's note "jump versus duck unhearable" and my S1-R line that left-right is "the one cue the evidence supports". The hop cut was unnecessary; my "thin game" objection stands.
