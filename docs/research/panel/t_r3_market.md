## Disputes

1. **Designer (r2 L104): "none reads head position with two hands".** Synth Riders does, hand-tracked, on a headset. Body-dodge plus hand-catch is VR's standard rhythm template; S2-R is that template played by ear. Boldest is "judged on originality".
2. **Designer (r2 L59), return.** Synth Riders lists removing obstacles and seated play as comfort settings (https://www.uploadvr.com/synth-riders-apple-vision-pro/): incumbents let seated players switch off the verb S2-R makes compulsory. Mainstream seated adults return occasionally to S2-R, rarely to M1-R (inference).
3. **Engineer (r1 L39): S2 Win 4.** No hand in the loop, and head-tilt dodging ships on Vision Pro and AirPods (t_r1_market [1][2]). Win 3.
4. **Designer (r2 L85), Homing.** Agreed it is weak; Papa Sangre makes it older than stated.

## Concessions

- **Platform test for S2-R: "still no" to "partly".** Earbuds report rotation, not position; a phone cannot run S2-R. S2 as described stays "no".
- **M1 Win 3 to 2.** Judge, engineer, skeptic: looking and steering collide; the designer cannot make it comfortable and competitive.
- **My S1 note "height unhearable" overstated.** Engineer: the browser renders elevation; pitch makes a two-way choice workable. S1 stays 3 for other reasons.
