## R1

- **Quest has it twice, same feature list.** Speed Cube (2021, $4.99, hands and controllers; "automatic shuffler and solver", "simple solving guider", leaderboards): 2.7 stars, 12 reviews in five years [1]. Cube Puzzle XR (2025, $3.99, passthrough, step-by-step tutorial, timer): too few reviews to rate [2].
- **Already built in this SDK.** Devpost "Rubiks Cube VR": WebXR plus IWSDK, pinch to turn layers, voice notation, timed solves [3, summary; fetch failed, hackathon and result unknown].
- **Vision Pro.** cuVision ($1.99, hand-tracked) and Cube Spatial (free) have too few ratings to display [4][5]. The one visible review: "swiping to turn faces is highly unpredictable, and there's no way to rotate the whole cube" [5]. No review praising hand turning was found.
- **Learning has owners.** GoCube / Rubik's Connected: a real Bluetooth cube mirrored in a phone app with interactive tutorials; about 2,300 iOS reviews at 4.6 [6, summary]. Camera-scan solvers are a phone commodity [7, summary].
- **Demand is real but off-headset.** About 500 million cubes sold [9]; over 290,000 World Cube Association competitors [10]; r/Cubers about 160k; a leading beginner tutorial has 33M to 76M views (sources disagree) [11, summary]. These people own cubes.
- **Name.** Spin Master bought the brand for US$50M [8, summary]. "Rubik" and "Rubik's" are registered marks; the EU shape mark fell; the patent expired in 2000 [9]. Store listings avoid the name [1][2][4][5]. Call it a cube puzzle.
- **Fall apart elsewhere?** No. It simulates a ten-dollar object.

## D1

- **Headsets: nothing found.** No sudoku of any kind surfaced on Quest in three searches (thin). 3Doku lists Apple Vision but is a 2011 iPad app with one US rating [12].
- **3D sudoku is fifteen years old without an audience.** 3Doku (9x9x9, 27 intersecting grids): reviewers say extra constraints make it "easier", it is long ("729 cells to fill") and "got tedious really fast" [12]. KSudoku's manual calls Roxdoku (3x3x3 to 5x5x5) "easier than they sound" [13]. Tredoku is a book series, 3D drawn flat [14, summary]. Sudokube 3D is a cube-twisting hybrid [15, summary].
- **Flat audience.** NYT: 11.2 billion game solves in 2025 [16, earlier report]; Easybrain (Sudoku.com): 1.5 billion portfolio downloads [17]; "over 100 million" regular players is an unsourced estimate [18, summary].
- **Meta's text points here.** Leisure Lovers play "accessible puzzle, strategy, or rhythm games", have "the shortest Quest sessions", "prioritize self improvement", are "underrepresented on Quest" and "primarily play games on their mobile devices" [19]. Caution: classic logic games in mixed reality draw little (Hand Puzzles 3.1 stars, 20 reviews [20]); the hit is an original spatial puzzle (Cubism 4.8, 1,074 ratings [21]).
- **Fall apart elsewhere?** Partly. A solid grid is what a flat screen shows worst; yet it has run on iPad and desktop for years.
