# Round 3: Competition Judge (R1, D1, R1-R, D1-R)

Criteria, Innovation/Experience/Technical/Polish: R1-R 4/7/6/6 (23); D1-R 6/6/6/6 (24).

## Disputes
1. **Designer, R1-R Win 5.** I give 4. Quest already has two hand-tracked cube tutors (market). Par puzzles and previewed turns improve on them, but a judge reads the same feature list, so Innovation stays 4. The pale, glyphed look and the name clear the brand rule (the store lists unbranded cubes), provided the brand never appears in text or video.
2. **Designer, Accessibility Forward as a second route.** Not credible: no voice, no eyes-only, untested, and one-hand pinch is what every gaze-and-pinch entry offers.
3. **Designer, D1-R Reason to Come Back "moderate".** Weak. Self-filling does answer "tedious" (twelve decisions, each paid with a chain), but the designer's script shows every puzzle falls to single-step logic, so "thoughtful progression" is missing, and a lone judge sees an empty shelf.
4. **Designer, 6x6x6 daily.** Eight minutes rests on two samples. Bead digits are legible at 3.2° cells; at six wide, tiered and foreshortened, colour and shape do the reading (inference; device check). Keep the judged path at 4x4x4.
5. **Market, D1 above R1.** As written D1 gives hands nothing; R1 stays marginally ahead.

## Concessions
- **First Five Minutes (to the designer).** "No beginner payoff in five minutes" holds for R1 only. Undoing three shown turns, then solving an unseen three-turn scramble with undo, is plausibly a real unaided win by minute three; the five-person playtest must confirm. The award is reachable on its wording; one wrong-layer turn loses it.
- **R1 Innovation 4 to 3, Feasibility 4 to 3.** I had not found the Quest apps; a shipped Vision Pro cube is reviewed as "highly unpredictable".
- **R1 Accessibility suggestion withdrawn.** "Eyes-only navigation" is unavailable: gaze is hidden until a pinch.
- **D1 award, Boldest (weak) to none.** It has run on iPad since 2011.
- **9x9x9.** Harmless if locked and built last; a visible locked marathon is D1-R's only "something to work toward".
- **Cube distance, 45 cm to 0.8 m.** Right for ray input; the cost is that hands no longer hold the object.
- **D1-R hands.** Pull-to-cut and flick are one pinch-drag verb inside the loop: more than N2's pointing, less than manipulation.
