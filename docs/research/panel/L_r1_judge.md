# Lamplight, round 1: Competition Judge

Quotes re-checked today (URLs at end). Judge behaviour and pool sizes are inference.

## 1. As written (26/40)

- **Stage 1: pass.** Near-verbatim track fit ("task management", "habits tied to a recurring context"). Hands-only holds only if the silent path is complete.
- **Innovation 7.** Hits "passthrough mode, scene understanding, persistent anchors) to drive repeat usage". Loses originality: speech-to-tasks is the obvious 2026 Productivity entry (inference); voice is not headset-specific.
- **Experience 7.** "A table becomes the play surface" and "habit-forming purpose" fit. Nine steps and five hand verbs fight Meta's "teach one core interaction, use it everywhere".
- **Technical 6.** "Solid and bug-free" and "holding up in rooms the developer never tested" depend on microphone permission, network, recognition and surface detection, all in minute one.
- **Polish 6.** The lamp's animation is the art direction and is unproven; feature count dilutes finish. Very filmable.
- **Win 6** (level with A4-R, below N3 at 7). **Feasibility 3. Difficulty 4.**

## 2. Routes, ranked

1. **Best New Productivity.** Two prizes, thinnest field (earlier inference), verbatim fit.
2. **Accessibility Forward.** "Voice alternatives for hand interactions, eyes-only navigation for limited mobility, high-contrast modes, or experiences inclusive by default." Voice already exists, so a complete hands-free path is nearly free. Least-targeted pool (inference). Judges will not find it unaided: one visible switch, ten seconds of video.
3. **Agentic.** "AI is a core part of the experience". The lamp is a "spatial agent", but one-shot speech-to-cards is formatting, not agency, and the spec's fallback shows the app works without it: good for the "take it away test", bad for "core". A judge who teaches "what an agentic interaction looks like when it's actually useful, where it tends to break" rewards bounded proposals the app executes, visible listening and thinking states, the audio snippet as provenance, by-hand repair, graceful offline behaviour; and punishes dead air, invented tasks, unfixable mishearing, anything chat-shaped. Large pool, one winner.
4. **First Five Minutes.** Excellent opener, but the payoff waits on speech plus a network round trip. By-product only.
5. **Reason to Come Back.** Adapted titles with real progression own this (inference); a sample-data week reads as a mock-up. Not worth chasing.

Features serving only weak routes: streak, cosmetic growth, weekly review, phrasing memory (Come Back); head nod (duplicates voice); time tags, spoken lamp voice (no rubric line).

## 3. Open office

Inference: most judges will not speak; some mutter a five-word test; none dictate a minute of real plans to an entrant's server. So the default path must be silent, hands-only and offline, and still show the lamp deciding something. Voice is an invitation after the first payoff, never a gate. A labelled "hear an example" should run a canned note through the real pipeline.

## 4. Engine

The rubric names "gaze interactions (Unity/Unreal ISDK v207+)" and "scene understanding (MRUK)", but the rules accept the web SDK by link, and I score behaviour, not SDK names; Quest 3 has no eye tracking in any engine. Engine moves Technical by about one point, through what I notice: microphone working in-session, the table found in my room, cards in place after relaunch. Which engine does these better is the engineer's to source. URL versus release-channel install: both official; I cannot source which costs judges more. "Do not make any modifications to your submission" also binds a server.

## 5. Keep / cut / change / add

**Keep**
1. Real desk, one persistent anchor, cards waiting ("persistent anchors ... repeat usage").
2. Lamp as body-language agent, bounded initiative, audio snippet per card (Agentic).
3. Chain pull and time-lapse to morning (First Five Minutes).

**Cut**
4. Streak, cosmetic growth, weekly review, phrasing memory ("scope to one mechanic and finish it").
5. Head nod, time tags, system keyboard, spoken voice ("solid and bug-free").
6. Fist crumple: two hand verbs at most, slide and press.

**Change**
7. First run silent and offline by default, sample cards already on the desk.
8. Suggestion of three becomes Core, shows a reason, works on any card: AI seen without speech.
9. Design for a five-second utterance with by-hand repair.
10. Lamp form: banker's lamp or lantern, not an articulated-arm character (third-party IP rule).

**Add**
11. One complete hands-free path, described in the award's words.
12. First-week device test of microphone, surface and anchor, settling the engine.

**Biggest movers:** 7, 8, and cuts 4 to 6 together. Expected after them: about 29/40, **Win 7**, Feasibility 4.
